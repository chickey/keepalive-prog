Not connected Firmware
This browser can't talk to the Keep Alive. Configuring the device needs Web Serial, which works in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer. You can still look around with Try demo.

Connect your Keep Alive

  1. Plug the Keep Alive into a USB port on this computer, with nothing plugged into its own socket.
  2. Click Connect and choose Eplop Keep Alive from the list.
  3. Adjust the three presets, then click Save to device.

Pulsing pauses while the Keep Alive is connected to a computer. Use Start trial to watch a preset run.

Need new firmware? Use the firmware flasher.

Presets

The button on the Keep Alive steps through these. Its light blinks once, twice or three times to show which is active. Untick Enabled on a preset to leave it out, for example to keep just one.

Light when running from a power bank

Whichever you choose, the light still blinks when you press the button. Off uses the least power.
